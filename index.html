— Our Story

A neighbourhood bistro built around a wood-fired oven and a stubborn love for the classics.

The Doughcrust Co began as a tiny pizza counter on the corner of a quiet street. Seven years and one stubborn oven later, it's a full bistro — serving slow-fermented dough, handcut pasta, smash burgers built on a screaming flat-top, and the kind of mocktails you'd actually order twice.

We don't chase trends. We chase good ingredients, fair sourcing, and a room that feels like it's been here forever. Sit down, stay a while. There's always a table for one more.
