— Our Story

A neighbourhood kitchen built around wood-fired dough and a stubborn love for bold flavours.

The Dough & Crust Co. began as a tiny pizza counter. Seven years and one stubborn oven later, it's a full kitchen — serving slow-fermented dough, hand-tossed pasta, smash burgers, loaded fries, refreshing mocktails, and milkshakes you'd actually order twice.

We don't chase trends. We chase good ingredients, bold flavours, and a room that feels like it's been here forever. Pull up a chair, stay a while. There's always room for one more.
